What 99% costs

Every point of service level is paid for in safety stock. Pick a target and see what it ties up for one example part: 120 sold a day, give or take 35, on a 9-day lead time, at $42 each.

Cycle service level with normally distributed demand: safety stock = z × 35 × √9. An example part, not client data.

173unitsof safety stock

$7,254of cash on the shelf

$1,813a year to hold it, at 25%
